## General info

My project is called “Genre Blend”. The aim of the project is to demonstrate a different approach towards the use of creative coding to blend different music genres creating a single unified composition that still retains a typical element for each genre.
 
The artistic challenge that I want to solve is the fusion of music genres that are almost opposite in terms of musical approach. Instead of simply placing two distinct genres side by side, I experimented with the ways in which the genres can interact with one another and join to form a blend.
 
In the music construction, I used two contrasting genres, house (Show Me Love) and classical music (Marvins Room), allowing me to experiment with the differences in rhythm, instruments, melody, and structure of music. The primary aim will not just be having two genres in one song, but to create a music process in which they influence each other.
